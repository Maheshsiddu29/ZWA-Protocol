## Frozen module and call map

Production's `LineageVerificationService` calls `ChainContextAuthenticator`, `RootAuthenticator`, and `TerminalAuthenticator` in `phase2.rs`. Their frozen Layer-B calls are:

- `chainref::ChainRef::decode/encode`, `finality::validate_identity`, `finality::validate_policy`, and `Tracker` for the chain context, finality, and reorg state;
- `root::authorize_root` for issuance root authentication, including `records_before`, `first_nullifier`, `path_from`, `policy_check`, Orchard issuance validation, and `util::shielded_sighash`;
- `terminal::authenticate_terminal` for Action leaf, Merkle path, ZSA bundle proof, and earlier-anchor checks;
- `view::ChainView::load`, `tx_leaves`, `new_tree`, plus `util::parse_block` and transaction Merkle hashing to re-derive an authenticated snapshot.

The research-only `build` and `object` modules and binaries are absent from the production core. `object` remains a **test oracle** for combined rows. `view::capture`, `util::rpc_call`, and `util::rpc` are the frozen network boundary: only `util.rs` directly names `reqwest`; `hyper` and `h2` enter transitively. Prover-side `root::make_evidence`, `terminal::make_evidence`, `view::witness/find`, and block-assembly/fixture helpers are excluded from the production core.
